HOW TO READ RESULTS

What do the risk levels mean?

RESULTSCOREWHAT IT MEANSWHAT TO DO
LOW RISK0–29 / 100Few warning signs were detected by this check.Still verify the sender or organization independently before sharing information or taking action.
REVIEW30–59 / 100Some warning signs were detected and need a closer look.Read the findings, check the details through official sources, and avoid clicking links or paying fees until verified.
HIGH RISK60–100 / 100Several or stronger warning signs were detected.Do not proceed or share sensitive information unless you confirm it is genuine through an official channel.

These are screening signals based on local rules and reports, not proof. A low-risk result does not guarantee that something is safe.
